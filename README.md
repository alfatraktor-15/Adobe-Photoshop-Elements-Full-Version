## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Comprehensive Editing Tools** — Access basic (rotate, flip, crop) and advanced tools (filters, area adjustments) to enhance your photos.
- **Photomerge Technology** — Create breathtaking panoramas and achieve incredible exposure by merging multiple photos.
- **People Recognition** — Automatically identify friends and family in your images for quick access.
- **RAW File Support** — Improved capabilities to work with RAW files for better editing precision.
- **Flexible Printing Options** — Print your creations easily with customizable settings.

### Advanced Features:
- **Automatic Tagging** — Smartly categorize your multimedia content for easy retrieval.
- **Simultaneous Preview & Edit** — View and edit images side by side for a seamless workflow.
- **Object Movement Function** — Easily move objects within your photos.
- **Red-Eye Correction** — Instantly remove red-eye effects for perfect portraits.
- **Social Media Sharing** — Effortlessly share your edited photos on platforms like Facebook and Twitter.
